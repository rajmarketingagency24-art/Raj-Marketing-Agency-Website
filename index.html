Our Story

Your Trusted Digital Marketing Partner in Telangana

Raj Marketing Agency was born from a simple dream — helping businesses grow. Founder Srikanth Raj discovered the power of digital promotion while marketing real estate with his father, using just his phone to create and publish property videos on YouTube.

The results were remarkable — multiple properties sold within months without a website or fancy tools. That sparked a deep dive into digital marketing, web development, graphic design, and content creation.

Today, we deliver complete digital solutions — websites, SEO, branding, ads, and more — at affordable monthly packages that fit every budget.
