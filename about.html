About Us

Our Story

Your Digital Growth Partner

Raj Marketing Agency was born from a simple dream — helping businesses grow.

Before the pandemic, founder Srikanth Raj and his father Raj Kumar worked as real estate consultants. While marketing properties, Srikanth discovered the power of digital promotion — using just his phone to shoot, edit, and publish property videos on YouTube.

The results were remarkable. Multiple properties sold within months, all without a website or fancy tools. That's when he knew digital marketing was the future.

He dove deep into digital marketing, web development, graphic design, and content creation — turning his skills into a mission to help businesses build a powerful online presence.

Today, Raj Marketing Agency delivers complete digital solutions — websites, SEO, branding, ads, and more — at affordable monthly packages that fit every budget.
