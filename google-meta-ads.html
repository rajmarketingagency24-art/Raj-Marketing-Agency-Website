FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about our Google & Meta Ads services.

How much budget is required for Google Ads?
The minimum budget depends on your industry, location, and goals. For local businesses in Sangareddy, we typically recommend starting with ₹15,000–₹25,000 per month for Google Search Ads. We'll help you find the right budget that balances visibility with ROI.
Which is better: Google Ads or Meta Ads?
Both platforms serve different purposes. Google Ads captures high-intent users actively searching for your services, while Meta Ads excels at building brand awareness and targeting users based on interests and behaviors. We often recommend a combined approach for the best results.
How long does it take to see results?
Google Ads can start generating clicks and leads within 24–48 hours of campaign launch. Meta Ads typically need 3–7 days for the algorithm to optimize delivery. Significant improvements in cost-per-lead and ROI usually stabilize within 2–4 weeks of active optimization.
Do you provide reports?
Yes! We provide weekly performance snapshots and detailed monthly reports with key metrics including impressions, clicks, conversions, cost-per-lead, ROI analysis, and actionable recommendations for improvement.
Can you optimize existing campaigns?
Absolutely. We perform a comprehensive audit of your existing campaigns — analyzing targeting, ad copy, keywords, bidding strategies, and conversion tracking — then implement optimizations to improve performance and reduce wasted spend.
